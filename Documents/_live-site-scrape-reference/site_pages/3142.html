WHO WE ARE

 

DIVINE FLAME AND ENERGY INTERNATIONAL LIMITED was incorporated in 2003 with a sole objective to impact and develop the engineering sector of the oil and gas industry. We are a fully integrated Oil and Gas Service organization with particular experience in Engineering, Procurement, Assemblage, Inspection, Repairs and Maintenance Management for Wellhead & Xmas Tree Equipment, Chokes, Valves, Flanges, Gaskets, Wellhead Control Panels, Actuators and Associated Equipment.

Our team comprises of highly qualified field service personnel with a wealth of experience in working with several reputable OEM equipment and are readily available for Onshore/Offshore deployments on 24/7 basis.

We adhere to proven industry engineering practices and our operations are based on ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001: 2018, API SPEC Q2 (API-6A, API-6D and API-17D) standards.
